Knife Terminal — Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Knife Terminal for iOS mirrors terminal sessions from the Knife Terminal Mac app. It is built so that we (CW&T) never see your data.

What the app stores, and where

The content of your terminal sessions — screen text, chat transcripts, project names, and anything you type — syncs between your Mac and your iPhone through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). This data is readable only by your Apple account. It never touches our servers, and we have no ability to access it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no accounts. We collect no personal information.

Notifications

Push notifications (for example, "a session is waiting for you") are delivered by Apple's push service, triggered by your own Mac through your private iCloud database.

Open source

The complete source code is public at github.com/cheewee2000/knife-terminal, so all of the above can be verified.
